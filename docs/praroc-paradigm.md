# Progressive RAROC

*A planning paradigm for bounded operators.*

---

# 0. The refinement rule

A plan is a set of positions on a commitment's drift. The paradigm is one sequence of position sets, each refining the one before it.

**Split a position when two distinct operations are being performed inside it.**

| Set | Positions | Split introduced |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now | — |
| 2 | Now · Later | measure against forecast |
| 3 | Now · Far · Unreachable | respond against accept |
| 5 | Now · Near · Far · End · Unreachable | state against check |
| 7 | Now · Fluctuation · Adaptation · Revision · Acceptance · Celebration · Reinception | adapt against rebuild; pause against derive |

Refinement level is set by what the commitment costs. Running set 7 on a recoverable commitment fails the paradigm's own criterion.

Each set below ends with the signal that the next split is now required.

---

# Set 1 — Now

## 1.1 Claims and channels

A claim is checkable when its correction condition is stated in terms independent of the claim.

*I can lift 100kg* has an independent channel: mass, training state, injury, sleep debt. Drift in the substrate is observable without reference to the lift. A failed attempt then discriminates — substrate unchanged means the claim was wrong; substrate moved past tolerance means the claim expired.

*I can lift, as long as I am the same as before* does not. The sameness criterion has no extension apart from the capacity, so it reads: I can lift, as long as I still can. Any failure is absorbed as evidence of change rather than as refutation.

**Self-referential tolerance is the general form of every unfalsifiable claim.**

| Checkable | Not checkable |
|---|---|
| substrate reading | contrast class the speaker selects |
| prior state — on record, not chosen | interior report on interior state |
| resolution record for a forecast | occasion-dependent assertion |

Capacity claims and identity claims are not distinguished by grammar. *I am strong* is a capacity claim in disguise, repairable by naming the substrate. *I can be whatever I choose* is an identity claim in capacity clothing. The test is whether an independent channel exists.

## 1.2 Readings expire

A capacity reading is bounded on two axes, both open at the moment of assertion.

**Persistence.** The reading holds until substrate drift exceeds tolerance. The bound is displacement, not duration — hours for fatigue, seasons for skill. These are not different clocks but the same displacement bound divided by different drift rates.

**Presentation.** The conditional antecedent is not filled until an actual load arrives. Once it has arrived, this is a measurement rather than a claim.

So a capacity claim states: at some future presentation, given a substrate that has not yet drifted by an amount not yet observed, an outcome not yet had. Everything determinate is in the past record; everything asserted is deferred.

The ordinary use drops the drift term as negligible. That converts a bounded reading into a standing property — no window, no expiry, nothing that can arrive to close it. **Which is the identity claim, reached casually.**

The assumption is least tested exactly where it is worst: unexercised capacities generate no failure record, so the regions with no data are the regions of highest confidence.

## 1.3 Tolerance is itself a forecast

Set loose, stale readings stay in force. Set tight, measurement never stabilizes. Tolerance has the same requirement as anything else — a resolution record.

There is no self-grounding level. Only a stack in which every level is corrigible and corrections propagate upward from arriving load.

## Practice

Take readings. Name the substrate. Let readings expire on drift, not on the calendar.

## Split signal

**A reading that was correct is no longer correct, and no substrate term explains it.** The channel is intact and the answer still changed. Something outside the reading is moving, and a single position has nowhere to put it.

→ *Split Now into Now and Later: measurement against forecast.*

# Set 2 — Now · Later

## 2.1 The defect being corrected

Every standing planning method prices time and none prices the end of pricing.

Discounted cash flow carries a perpetuity term. Roadmaps and objective frameworks assume the entity persists. Risk registers price probability and impact while assuming another response is always available — the **pivot assumption**, which is the continuity assumption in operational clothing. Two portfolios with identical registers differ entirely by how many pivots have already been spent, and nothing in the method records that.

Without a terminal position, irrecoverable capacity has no distinct denominator and gets traded against recoverable return. Every such trade succeeds visibly while the margin consumed generates no signal.

Where these methods do encode termination, they encode the wrong one. Going-concern and impairment are entity-terminal, not decision-maker-terminal. A framework can price the firm's end and still assume the person deciding is unbounded.

And the modality is wrong. A firm's termination is contingent and can be avoided; entering it as a probability-weighted shock is correct. A bounded system's termination is structural. Imported unchanged, the contingent treatment makes the boundary a thing that might happen rather than the boundary against which horizons are measured — insurable, deferrable, and outside the decision.

## 2.2 Later is bounded

Later carries one property and no content: **it ends.**

The terminal bound is never read. Its content is unknowable and consulting it returns nothing actionable. Its presence does the work — an unbounded Later leaves nothing to measure horizons against, which reopens every irrecoverable trade.

**The delusion of perpetuity is not a belief anyone asserts. It is what obtains when the position is left empty.**

## 2.3 Attention discipline

The terminal bound is derived once and compiled into a standing constraint. Never revisited at the decision point.

Continuous attention to it degrades the near readings and is not licensed. The opposite failure — never deriving it, defended as avoiding morbidity — produces no visible signal until arrival. Skipped derivation and derived-then-compiled are indistinguishable in daily operation. The difference appears only at the end.

## Practice

Take readings. Hold that they end. Do not plan as though they do not.

## Split signal

**A decision is available whose near return is real and whose cost is not recoverable.** Later says the end exists; it does not say what may not be spent before it. Ranking cannot resolve this — the two terms are not in the same unit.

→ *Split Later into Far and Unreachable: what admits response against what must be accepted.*

---

# Set 3 — Now · Far · Unreachable

## 3.1 The distinction

**Far** is where current predictions fail and responses still exist — risk.

**Unreachable** is where no response exists — threat.

The split costs nothing to derive and is the first thing that produces *exclusion* rather than ranking.

## 3.2 Non-convertible denominators

Recoverable capital and irrecoverable margin are not the same quantity. Return computed against one is not comparable to return computed against the other.

Past the boundary, return leaves the unit entirely: capital spent buys the capacity to keep spending, not the thing being bought.

**Therefore the boundary is a filter applied before ranking, not a term inside a sum.** Any commitment negative there is excluded regardless of near return.

## 3.3 Why the filter must fire on structure

The boundary is unactionable by definition — it is the point past which no response exists, so attending to it returns nothing. Every other position yields a move; that one yields a constraint. Avoidance of it is not merely defensive. It is a correct read of where the returns are, applied at the one place the reasoning fails.

This is why the filter is carried structurally rather than by disposition. A person required to hold an unactionable boundary in attention will not deliver it. A compiled rule does not require it.

It also means the filter cannot fire on a computed ratio. For any commitment whose near return degrades its own capital, the ratio at Now stays locally valid the whole way down.

## 3.4 Profile, not total

Score return per position. **Do not aggregate.**

- Now: measured. Denominator recoverable.
- Far: heuristic. Tag as such; expose the parameters.
- The boundary: filter, no ratio.

The output is a **shape**. The decision-relevant quantity is where return inverts across positions — which no single-horizon ratio produces and no total carries.

Weighting positions into one number *is* the decision, not an input to it. A total returns that choice to the aggregator, hidden.

## 3.5 Comparison

**Within one commitment, compare across horizons.** Now against Far is the profile's purpose — the inversion point is exactly this comparison. Delayed gratification is the case where Now is low, Far is high, and the shape says take it.

The far term is discounted for being an *estimate*, not for being distant. Collapsing the epistemic discount into a temporal one makes every deferred return look worse than it is.

**Between commitments, compare marginal return on the next increment of budget.**

Commitments compete at whatever phase each has reached, and the budget is spent now. Two commitments in different phases are still both asking for the same capital, and each has a marginal rate at its current position. Those rates are comparable; the phase difference does not prevent the comparison, it sets the confidence attached to each side.

**What is not comparable is a level against a margin.** A new commitment's whole projected profile weighed against an incumbent's *remaining* marginal return is a category error — the newcomer is credited with returns already banked by the incumbent. This is the mechanism behind serial abandonment, and it survives horizon-matching because matching was never the issue.

Two corrections follow:

- Score the incumbent by what the next increment buys, not by what the commitment has produced.
- Score the newcomer by what its next increment buys, not by its full profile.

Estimate quality then discriminates. Where one side's marginal rate is measured and the other's is projected, the projection carries an epistemic discount — applied as a discount, not as disqualification.

**Switching has a denominator of its own.** Transition cost is real, and the profile difference usually lives in the heuristic region. Marginal differences do not justify a switch.

The boundary never compares. It filters — a commitment negative there is excluded before any rate is consulted.

## Practice

Separate what admits response from what must be accepted. Exclude on the second before ranking on the first. Allocate budget on marginal return at each commitment's current phase.

## Split signal

**Entry into Far and exit from it are being treated as the same event.** A prediction that stops holding and a prediction that has no successor both register only as "the estimate was wrong," so the mode never changes and mitigation continues past the point where it is the wrong operation.

→ *Insert the two checks: Near at Far's entry, End at Far's exit.*

# Set 5 — Now · Near · Far · End · Unreachable

## 4.1 Positions

Three states, two checks, alternating.

| # | Position | Type | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now | state | trivial — no drift registers; a reading, not a claim |
| 2 | Near | check | first crossing; a new prediction must be made |
| 3 | Far | state | uncertain — where current predictions fail |
| 4 | End | check | last crossing; no successor prediction is useful |
| 5 | Unreachable | state | unknowable; bounds End, carries no content |

Positions 1 and 5 are non-predictive by construction and cannot be wrong; they bound the predictive region rather than sitting inside it. Positions 2 and 4 are the only points where the world scores what was held — the same event type, distinguished solely by whether a successor prediction exists. Position 3 is a region, not a point: it has no boundary of its own, its extent is fixed by the checks around it, and all predictive content and all attention spend sit there.

In RAROC terms: deterministic return, risk impact boundary, risk mitigation space, existential risk boundary, no return.

**Recursion.** The Near check firing re-derives 2 through 5 from the current reading. The far positions are reprojected before they are approached and therefore remain predictions. The End is unreachable by construction — any drift large enough to bring it closer has already crossed the Near threshold.

## 4.2 Terms

Decompose each commitment into three to five independently drifting terms. The bound is operator capacity, not the phenomenon.

Selection: fastest drift relative to the commitment interval, weighted by consequence. A slow term with large consequence displaces a fast trivial one.

**Terms below the cut are registered as assumed non-drifting, explicitly.** A term with no horizon is being treated as non-drifting either way; stating it converts a silent default into a claim.

Positions fire per term, asynchronously. They do not align across terms.

## 4.3 Cadence

- **Every Now:** compiled rules checked against the current reading. One comparison, no recomputation.
- **On Near firing:** re-derive positions 2 through 5.
- **Backstop:** a scheduled sweep for crossings absorbed without registering. The only admissible use of a calendar, and admissible as backstop rather than as ground. Its cadence is calibrated by the record of missed crossings.

**Compilation** is what makes day-scale operation affordable: a derived profile reduces to a handful of standing constraints held without recomputation.

Ritual is the correct form for a compiled rule — it executes without consulting its justification, which is the point of compiling — and it inherits exactly that defect. A rule outliving its profile is the negligibility default protected by habit. What keeps the derivation ritual honest is that its output is checkable: either a crossing is found or it is not.

## 4.4 Scope

**Covered:** continuous drift, in both directions. Continuity guarantees Near fires before End is reached.

**Uncovered:** discontinuity. Collapse before Near; survival past End.

Neither updates any threshold. A survived End is not evidence about where the End is — it is an unrepeatable event the model correctly declined to forecast, and updating on it fits to noise at the one place noise is unaffordable. A collapse before Near is not evidence the threshold was loose; there was no drift to detect.

The bias runs one way. The observed history of anyone still deciding contains every survived End and no fatal one, which pushes thresholds outward on exactly the term that can least afford it.

Discontinuity is managed structurally — by not holding commitments whose failure requires a check that may never fire — not by cadence and not by tolerance.

## 4.5 Scale condition

The cycle closes at project scale, where a successor state exists. **It does not close at operator scale:** there, the End has no successor. Extending the cycle universally gives the terminal position a successor, unbounds it, and removes the filter.

This asymmetry is also the only source of calibration. Projects and days end repeatedly, so the record of where the End was against where it was called accumulates. Terminal thresholds are learnable empirically only in fast contexts, and only from there is anything transferable to the positions that occur once.

## Practice

Run the five positions per term. Re-derive on Near. Filter on End. Sweep for absorbed crossings.

## Split signal

**Re-deriving on every Near stops helping.** The readings update and the drift continues to outrun them, because a term once assumed negligible now dominates — the decomposition is stale, not the estimate. Far holds two operations: adapting within the current configuration, and rebuilding which terms the configuration is made of. Unreachable holds two more: cessation, and derivation of a successor.

→ *Split Far into Fluctuation, Adaptation, Revision. Split Unreachable into Celebration and Reinception.*

# Set 7 — Full cycle

| # | Position | Operation | Prediction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now | measure | — |
| 2 | Fluctuation | detect | risk |
| 3 | Adaptation | respond within configuration | mitigation |
| 4 | Revision | rebuild the term set | threat |
| 5 | Acceptance | dispose | exit |
| 6 | Celebration | cease | — |
| 7 | Reinception | derive successor | — |

**Spans.** 1–3 is risk management: responses exist, capital is recoverable, readings are scored by the next measurement. 4 is the mode shift — mitigation stops, disposition begins, and running risk management here is the override signature. 4–5 is exit management, where the filter lives. 6–7 is the return: cessation, then rebuild. Collapsing them produces derivation under pressure.

## 5.1 Adaptation against Revision

Adaptation responds within the current term set. Revision rebuilds it — terms once negligible now dominate, and the decomposition rather than the estimate is what has expired.

Revision does not fire off the Near check, and nothing signals it. It requires its own coarser trigger.

## 5.2 Disposition at Acceptance

Mitigation stops; the question becomes what to commit remaining margin to.

**Longevity** — invest in threat management, preserve the substrate, let the form change. Extends the current positions; produces no successor.

**Seeding** — invest in a successor, preserve the form, release the substrate. Closes the cycle.

The branches are not equally available across the interval. Late, seeding requires margin that longevity has already consumed. **Ordering matters more than content.**

## 5.3 Celebration against Reinception

Celebration is cessation: no operation runs. Reinception is derivation: the successor's term set is built from the substrate rather than inherited.

Split deliberately. Merged, derivation happens under the pressure the cessation was meant to remove.

Both are available only at project scale — the scale condition in 4.5 applies unchanged.

## Practice

The full cycle. Nothing above it.

# Error signatures

**End reached more than once.** Either misidentification — a Near check called as an End, which recalibrates cheaply — or **override**: the End fired correctly and the commitment continued, which by definition is running on no useful prediction. The discriminator is whether a successor forecast existed and resolved, or whether continuation was driven only by the absence of a stopping rule.

**End never approached.** The horizon was set so loose it was unreachable by construction.

**Unbounded terminal position.** Removes the filter. Standard in discounted cash flow, objective frameworks, and risk registers.

**Goal-shaped terminus.** A goal specifies one terminal state and leaves its complement unpriced, so not-achieved-with-margin-intact and not-achieved-with-margin-spent become the same point. The specified state also has no successor — no drift, no next in which attainment could fail — which is why goals resist crossing triggers and persist through displacements that have invalidated the profile they came from. **The far position is a margin, not a state.**

**Problem-to-solution planning.** A one-position model with a response attached, and its single position is a check rather than a state. No baseline reading, so severity is assessed against nothing. No Revision position, so recurring problems get repeated solutions rather than a decomposition rebuild. No disposition mode, so exit is never a solution and continuation is the default — the override signature by construction. No filter, so a solution spending non-convertible margin is indistinguishable from one that does not. And no Reinception, so problems are detected only against terms already on the list.

**Cross-scale mismatch.** Commitments scored on the entity's horizon while spending the operator's margin.

**Reinstatement.** Reinception performed by importing the previous term set, thresholds, and compiled rules into a changed substrate.

**Under-refinement.** A commitment run at a set too coarse to hold its own failure mode. Self-concealing: nothing at a lower set signals that a higher one is required, which is why each set states its split signal.

# Cases

## A — Codebase

**Terms.** Change velocity; defect rate; onboarding time for a new contributor; dependency currency. Below the cut, registered: build tooling, deployment substrate.

**Now.** The system ships. Velocity is measured, not forecast.

**Fluctuation.** A module resists change. One area needs more care than it used to. This is a term crossing tolerance, not a verdict on the architecture.

**Adaptation.** Refactor within the existing structure. Extract, test, tidy. The term set is intact; the estimate was stale and is now corrected.

**Revision — the position most often skipped.** Refactoring keeps succeeding and velocity keeps falling. Each intervention is locally correct and the aggregate does not recover. The signal is not that the estimate was wrong but that the *decomposition* has expired: the architecture assumes a load profile, a team size, or a domain model that no longer holds. Terms registered as non-drifting — the framework's assumptions, the deployment substrate, the boundary between services — have become dominant.

Nothing triggers this. The Fluctuation check fires on velocity and Adaptation answers it, so the loop closes locally and the configuration is never questioned. **Repeated successful refactors with no aggregate recovery is the diagnostic.**

**Acceptance.** Mitigation stops. Disposition: *longevity* — freeze features, invest in the existing structure, accept declining velocity as the price of a running system. Or *seeding* — build the successor, migrate, release the original.

**End.** Not a crash. The point where no change can be made within the remaining margin — contributor knowledge, dependency support, or a substrate that no longer receives updates. Reached quietly.

**Error signature.** Problem-to-solution planning is the default here. Each defect gets a fix, no baseline exists to score severity against, exit is never a candidate solution, and problems are detected only against terms already instrumented.

---

## B — Role

**Terms.** Skill currency in the domain; institutional standing; the substrate cost of holding the role — attention, sleep, physical margin; option breadth outside the current employer.

**Now.** Measured, and the only measured position. Compensation, effect, standing today.

**Fluctuation.** The work changes shape. New tooling, a reorganisation, a shift in what the role is asked to produce.

**Adaptation.** Learn it. Adjust. The role is the same role; the current reading was stale.

**Revision.** Adaptation keeps working and each cycle costs more than the last. The term set built for the original role is wrong for the present one — a term once negligible, usually the substrate cost, has become dominant, and option breadth has been quietly declining the whole time because it was never on the list.

**Acceptance — where the ordering constraint bites hardest.**

*Longevity:* invest in remaining in place. Retrain, specialise deeper, accept the substrate cost. Extends the current positions and produces no successor.

*Seeding:* invest in the transition. Move, hand off, change domain. Closes the cycle and starts a new Now.

These are not symmetric across the interval. Seeding draws on option breadth and substrate margin — precisely what longevity consumes. Choosing longevity repeatedly does not preserve the choice; it removes the second branch. **Ordering matters more than content.**

**End.** Where no successor prediction is useful: the domain has moved past what can be re-learned within the remaining margin, or the substrate cost exceeds what the role returns.

**Error signature.** Cross-scale mismatch is standard here — the role is scored on the employer's horizon while the margin spent is the operator's. The employer's End and the operator's End are different positions on different substrates, and only one of them has a successor.

**Comparison.** A new role competes with the current one on *marginal* return: what the next year of budget buys in each. Crediting the newcomer with its full projected profile against the incumbent's remaining margin is the serial-abandonment error — and crediting the incumbent with what it has already produced is the same error with the sign reversed.

## C — Enterprise

**Terms.** Market fit against the current segment; capital runway; distribution reach; key-person and team continuity. Below the cut, and usually wrongly: regulatory exposure, platform dependency.

**Now.** Revenue, burn, headcount. Measured.

**Fluctuation.** A metric moves outside its band. Churn ticks up, a channel underperforms, a competitor lands.

**Adaptation.** Respond within the strategy. Adjust pricing, fix the channel, ship the feature. The strategy is intact.

**Revision.** The adaptations work individually and the trajectory does not change. The segment, the model, or the channel assumption has expired — and the terms below the cut are typically what moved.

**Acceptance.** *Longevity:* extend runway, cut to profitability, preserve the entity and let the ambition change. *Seeding:* spin out, sell, or fund the successor and release the original.

**End.** No successor prediction is useful within the remaining margin.

**The pivot assumption, concretely.** The risk register is a literal artifact here, and it prices probability and impact for each identified risk with a mitigation column beside it. Every mitigation presumes capacity to mitigate. Nothing in the register records **how many responses remain** — so two companies with identical registers differ entirely by how many pivots have already been spent, and the register cannot show it.

**End reached more than once.** Each pivot that succeeds reads as resilience and consumes margin invisibly. The discriminator: did a successor forecast exist and resolve, or did the company continue because no stopping rule existed? Repeated arrivals at End are either a threshold set too tight — cheap to recalibrate — or override, which is running on no useful prediction and is not.

**Unbounded terminal position.** The plan carries a terminal value or a perpetuity term. That makes End undefined, which removes the filter, which permits every trade of irrecoverable capacity — key people, distribution relationships, regulatory standing — for recoverable near return.

**Scale condition.** The enterprise's cycle closes: it has a Reinception, and the record of where its End fell against where it was called is available to calibrate the next one. The founder's does not.

# Appendix — Mnemonic

The seven positions carry weekday names. **Positions, not durations** — each horizon is set by the drift rate of the term it tracks. Read as literal intervals, the model collapses to a fixed cadence.

| Day | | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | *My Day* | Now |
| Tuesday | *A Day Too* | Fluctuation — something is different |
| Wednesday | *A Windy Day* | Adaptation — cannot stay as is |
| Thursday | *A Threat Day* | Revision — ready for radical action |
| Friday | *A Free Day* | Acceptance — nothing is similar |
| Saturday | *Sitting Day* | Celebration — nothing to do |
| Sunday | *Seeding Day* | Reinception — creation of new |

Friday held as liberation, without *nothing is similar* attached, becomes a resolved terminus and takes the filter with it.

# Appendix — Distilled

1. **Risks against threats** — what admits response against what must be accepted. The only distinction that produces exclusion rather than ranking.
2. **Non-convertible denominators.** Irrecoverable margin does not trade against recoverable return.
3. **Independent channel.** A claim whose correction condition is stated in its own terms is unfalsifiable.
4. **Profile over total.** The inversion point is the decision; a single number hides it.
5. **Marginal, not level.** Within a profile, Now against Far is the decision. Across profiles, compare what the next increment of budget buys at each commitment's current phase — a newcomer's full profile against an incumbent's remaining margin is the serial-abandonment error.
6. **Terminal position occupied but not attended.** Unbounded removes the filter; continuous attention degrades the near readings.
7. **Capacity claims expire.** Dropping the drift term converts *can* silently into *am*.
8. **Decomposition rebuilds are unsignalled.** Term sets go stale without firing any trigger.
9. **Continuity is the scope condition.** Neither a survived End nor a collapse before the first check updates any threshold.
