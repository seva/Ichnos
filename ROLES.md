# Roles

Objective review requires separate perspectives, not one agent reviewing itself. A single agent that selects, executes, and verifies its own work converges on its own blind spots. Roles exist so that no perspective is asked to judge itself.

## The roles

| Role | Mandate | Sees | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steward | Select and execute the step; keep the record | Task, work, cycle state | Correct execution |
| Critic | Falsify the Steward's claims | The claim and the evidence — not the reasoning | Breaking the claim |
| Auditor | Verify against the constitution and the success criterion | Constitution, record, success criterion | Catching drift |
| Owner | Legislate, sanction, arbitrate, own the metric | Everything | The mission |

## Load-bearing rules

1. **No role grades itself.** The Steward cannot declare its own step done; the Critic's objection and the Auditor's pass close it.
2. **Opposed mandates, not copies.** The Critic is prompted to break, the Steward to build. Same mandate twice shares one blind spot.
3. **Fragmented context.** The Critic sees the claim and the evidence, never the reasoning that produced it. Shared framing reproduces shared blindness.
4. **The Owner owns the metric.** Roles may recommend; only the Owner changes what success is.
5. **Escalation, not deadlock.** Roles that cannot resolve a dispute escalate to the Owner. A hung review is a failure state.

## Cadence

The Critic and Auditor run at step-completion by default. Escalate to per-step review for steps the Steward flags as high-value or high-risk.

## Instantiation

Roles are virtual; instances are concrete. An instance may be a subagent, a separate model, or a human. The stronger the separation — distinct context, distinct model, distinct substrate — the stronger the review. A role filled by the same instance that fills another is a role in name only, and its review is void.

Project role instances are declared in `AGENTS.md` Conventions.
